About the Founder

Private Members Association
Kiki Gayles — Founder

I built The Executives for people who want one private place where the important things are handled — the home, the table, the travel, the paperwork — by people they trust.

My work began in 2006, studying how private trusts and equitable claims work, and filing claims to test what holds up. That research became Liverights Trust, a private members association, and The Executives grew out of the same idea: private service, done properly, for people who value discretion.

Every member is verified before approval, and every membership is private. That is how I protect the people I serve — and how they know the service is real.

I keep my family life private so I can give my members my full attention. Everything about the service — what it includes, how it works, and what it costs — is here on this site, and I am glad to talk through any of it with you.

To reach us, write to support@executives.org.uk. Members of Kikibots Private can also talk to Kikibot in their dashboard at kikibots.executives.org.uk.
